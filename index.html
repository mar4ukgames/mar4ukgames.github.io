Why it fools your brain

Stroop effect

Your brain reads words before it sees colors, so a red button that says “BLUE” slows you down.

Go / No-Go

After three “tap yellow”, a sudden “don't tap yellow” is surprisingly hard to obey.

Task switching

Colors, numbers, sizes, voice traps — every rule change costs you a split second.

Modes

Classic, Hardcore with one life, and a Daily Challenge with the same tasks for everyone.

Challenge a friend

Send a code — your friend plays the exact same tasks and tries to beat your score.

Rewards

Daily quests, a 7-day login reward, achievements, leaderboards and a shop full of themes.
