Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

English Українська

In short: the developer of Colors Lie does not collect, store or sell your personal data. Your scores and settings stay on your phone. Ads (Google AdMob) and, where available, Google Play Games are provided by Google, which processes some data under its own privacy policy.

Who we are

Colors Lie (the "game") is a mobile reaction game published on Google Play under the package name com.marchuk.colorslie by an independent developer ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, contact us at the address in the Contact section below.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only in its private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us.

  • Game statistics: best score, last score, best combo, games played, total correct answers.
  • Daily challenge result for the current day.
  • Settings: language, sound, vibration and colorblind mode.

You can clear statistics in Settings → Reset statistics. Uninstalling the game deletes all of this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows only optional rewarded video ads: you choose to watch a video to get an extra life. There are no banners or pop-up ads. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob, which automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

DataExample
Device identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID
Approximate locationEstimated from your IP address
Interactions with adsAd views, taps, video completion
DiagnosticsApp and SDK performance data

This data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your consent for personalized ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Google Play Games

Where Google Play Games features (leaderboards) are available and you sign in, Google processes your Play Games profile name, avatar and the scores you submit, and shows them on leaderboards according to your profile visibility settings. You can manage your profile in the Google Play Games app. We do not receive your email address or contacts.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game. For data processed by Google, use your Google Account settings or Google's tools linked above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store your data, these requests are handled through the settings above or directly with Google. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. The current version is always available at this address.
